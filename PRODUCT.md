# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Technical users (developers, researchers, content workers) who need to extract text from PDFs into structured Markdown. They're at their desk, focused on a conversion task, and want it to just work.

## Product Purpose
Convert PDF documents to structured Markdown via GLM-OCR. Upload a PDF, get back clean Markdown. No accounts, no complexity.

## Brand Personality
Quiet, precise, utilitarian. No decoration for its own sake.

## Anti-references
SaaS dashboards with metric cards and gradient accents. Glassmorphism. Chat-style interfaces. Anything that looks like a marketing page.

## Design Principles
- Every pixel earns its place. If it doesn't serve the task, remove it.
- Typography and spacing do the visual work. Color is functional, not decorative.
- The interface should feel like a well-made tool, not a website.
- Progressive disclosure: show only what's needed for the current step.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA minimum. Respect reduced motion. Sufficient contrast on all text.
